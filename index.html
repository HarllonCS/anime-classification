My list of the best anime of all time

3 best anime

1) Naruto

The story of Naruto follows Naruto Uzumaki, a young orphan from the Leaf Village who dreams of becoming the Hokage, the supreme leader and strongest ninja of his village.

2) Dragon Ball (classic)

Dragon Ball chronicles Son Goku's journey from childhood to adulthood as he learns martial arts, makes friends, and protects the planet from powerful threats.

3) Death Note

The anime's story revolves around Light Yagami, a brilliant high school student who finds a supernatural notebook called the Death Note.
